## 🔍 Explore (12 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Leaf Hunt | Go outdoors and find three different kinds of leaves. Bring them home, stick them on paper, and write their names with your Big Person's help! |
| 2 | Harbour Adventure | Go to the harbour and spot three different types of boat and two types of bird. Take pictures and print them for your Explore scrapbook! |
| 3 | Bug Safari | Go to the park with a magnifying glass. Find three different creepy-crawlies hiding under rocks or logs. Draw pictures of what you find! |
| 4 | Cloud Spotter | Lie on a blanket outside and look at the clouds. Can you find shapes that look like animals? Draw three cloud animals when you get home! |
| 5 | Colour Walk | Go for a walk and try to find something in nature for every colour of the rainbow. Collect safe items or take pictures! |
| 6 | Puddle Patrol | Put on your wellies and find puddles! Splash, measure how deep they are with a stick, and drop leaves in to make boats! |
| 7 | Texture Explorer | Find five things outside that feel different - smooth, rough, soft, bumpy, sticky. Make rubbings with paper and crayons! |
| 8 | Sound Detective | Sit quietly in the park for two minutes. How many different sounds can you hear? Birds? Wind? Dogs? Count them all! |
| 9 | Shadow Catcher | On a sunny day, trace your shadow with chalk on the pavement. Come back later - has it moved? Draw it again! |
| 10 | Rock Collection | Find five interesting rocks on your walk. Wash them at home, paint faces on them, and give each rock a name! |
| 11 | Flower Spotter | How many different coloured flowers can you find in your neighbourhood? Draw each one and try to find out its name! |
| 12 | Bridge Counter | Walk along the harbour and count all the bridges you can see! Take a photo on each one and make a bridge book! |

## 🧪 Experiment (12 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Volcano Fizz! | Make a volcano with baking soda and vinegar! Build a mountain from playdough, add baking soda inside, then pour in vinegar and watch it erupt! |
| 2 | Magic Milk | Pour milk in a plate, add drops of food colouring, then touch it with a cotton bud dipped in washing-up liquid. Watch the colours dance! |
| 3 | Sink or Float? | Fill a bowl with water. Collect toys and kitchen items. Guess which ones will sink and which will float, then test them! |
| 4 | Rainbow Walk | Put white kitchen paper in a glass of water with food colouring. Watch the colour walk up the paper like magic! Try different colours! |
| 5 | Bread Chewing Science | Chew a piece of bread for a really long time. Does it start tasting sweet? That's your mouth's special helpers turning bread into sugar! |
| 6 | Ice Rescue | Freeze small toys in a container of water. Use warm water, salt, and tools to rescue the toys from the ice! |
| 7 | Raisin Dancers | Drop raisins into a glass of fizzy water and watch them dance up and down! The bubbles carry them up like tiny lifts! |
| 8 | Magnet Explorer | Take a magnet around the house. What sticks? What doesn't? Sort your findings into two groups and talk about why! |
| 9 | Colour Mixing | With just red, yellow, and blue paint, try to make as many new colours as you can. What happens when you mix them all together? |
| 10 | Seed Grower | Put a wet cotton ball in a clear cup and press a bean seed against the side. Watch it sprout roots and leaves over the next week! |
| 11 | Sound Shakers | Fill containers with rice, pasta, beans, and sand. Shake them! Which is loudest? Quietest? Can you put them in order? |
| 12 | Static Hair | Rub a balloon on your jumper and hold it near your hair. What happens? Try it near tiny bits of paper too! |

## 💃 Dance (10 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Animal Dance Party | Put on your favourite song and dance like different animals! Stomp like an elephant, hop like a bunny, slither like a snake! |
| 2 | Freeze Dance | Play music and dance as wildly as you can. When the music stops - FREEZE! Hold your pose like a statue. Can your Big Person catch you moving? |
| 3 | Scarf Dancing | Grab a scarf or ribbon and put on some music. Make it flow and swirl. Try fast music and slow music - how does your scarf dance change? |
| 4 | Copy Cat Moves | Take turns with your Big Person. One person does a dance move, the other copies it. Keep adding moves to make a longer and longer dance! |
| 5 | Bubble Dance | Someone blows bubbles while you dance to pop them! Try to pop them with different body parts - elbows, knees, nose! |
| 6 | Slow Mo Disco | Try dancing in super slow motion like you're moving through jelly! Then speed up as fast as you can! Slow... fast... slow... fast! |
| 7 | Musical Feelings | Play different types of music - happy, gentle, bouncy, dramatic. How does each one make you want to move? Show the feeling! |
| 8 | Dance Story | Make up a dance that tells a story! Maybe you're a seed growing into a flower, or a rocket launching into space. Show it to your family! |
| 9 | Shadow Dance | Shine a torch on the wall and dance to make your shadow move! Can you make your shadow look like it's flying? |
| 10 | Rhythm Maker | Clap a pattern and stomp your feet to match. Can you make a beat? Tap your knees, clap your hands, stomp your feet - make your own song! |

## 🧩 Puzzle (10 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Shape Hunter | Walk around your home and find circles, squares, triangles, and rectangles. How many of each can you find? Draw a map of where they are! |
| 2 | Matching Pairs | Collect pairs of socks and mix them up. Can you find all the matching pairs? Time yourself and try to beat your record! |
| 3 | Pattern Builder | Use coloured blocks, buttons, or fruit to make a pattern. Red-blue-red-blue. Can you make a harder pattern? Can your Big Person guess what comes next? |
| 4 | Sorting Challenge | Grab a handful of different items. Sort them by colour, then by size, then by shape. How many different ways can you sort the same things? |
| 5 | Treasure Map | Your Big Person hides a toy and draws a simple map with clues. Follow the map to find the treasure! Then make a map for them! |
| 6 | What's Missing? | Put five toys on a tray. Look carefully, then close your eyes. Your Big Person takes one away. Can you spot what's missing? Add more toys to make it harder! |
| 7 | Counting Quest | How many steps from your bedroom to the kitchen? How many spoons in the drawer? How many books on the shelf? Count everything! |
| 8 | Story Sequencing | Draw three pictures that tell a story - beginning, middle, and end. Mix them up and put them back in the right order! |
| 9 | Mirror Puzzle | Your Big Person makes a shape or pattern with blocks on one side. You try to build the exact mirror image on the other side! |
| 10 | Odd One Out | Your Big Person puts out groups of things where one doesn't belong. A fork among spoons! A red block among blue ones! Find the odd one out! |

## 🧱 Build (10 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Tallest Tower | How tall can you build a tower with Duplo? Measure it against yourself! Can you make it taller than your tummy? Your shoulders? Your head? |
| 2 | Animal House | Build a home for your favourite toy animal using Duplo or Magnetiles. It needs a door, a window, and a roof. Give your animal a tour! |
| 3 | Bridge Builder | Build a bridge with Magnetiles or Duplo that a toy car can drive under. Make it as long as you can without it falling down! |
| 4 | Castle Creator | Build a castle with towers and walls. Use Duplo for the base and Magnetiles for the roofs. Who lives in your castle? |
| 5 | Marble Run | Use cardboard tubes, boxes, and tape to make a ramp for a ball or marble. How far can you make it roll? Add twists and turns! |
| 6 | Robot Friend | Build a robot from boxes, cups, and tape. Give it a name, draw its face, and tell everyone what your robot can do! |
| 7 | Train Track Town | Build a track and then build a whole town around it with blocks. Add houses, a shop, a park, and a station! |
| 8 | Blanket Fort | Build a cozy den using cushions, blankets, and chairs. Bring in books, a torch, and a snack. Read a story in your fort! |
| 9 | Boat Builder | Make a boat from foil, cork, or a plastic tub. Test it in the bath or a bowl of water. Can it carry small toys without sinking? |
| 10 | Magnetic Shapes | Use Magnetiles to make flat shapes on the floor - a star, a house, a flower. Then fold them up into 3D! What does your flat shape become? |

## 🎨 Paint (12 activities)

| # | Title | Description |
|---|-------|-------------|
| 1 | Handprint Animals | Dip your hands in paint and make handprints. Turn them into animals - a handprint can become a peacock, a tree, an octopus! How many can you make? |
| 2 | Blow Painting | Drop watery paint onto paper and blow it with a straw to make wild patterns. Use different colours and see what creatures or trees you can see in the splatters! |
| 3 | Nature Art | Collect leaves, sticks, and petals from outside. Arrange them into a picture on a piece of card and stick them down. A leaf butterfly? A stick person? |
| 4 | Bubble Wrap Stamping | Wrap bubble wrap around a rolling pin or your hand. Dip it in paint and stamp it on paper for amazing textures! Make a bubble wrap caterpillar! |
| 5 | Colour Mixing Magic | Start with only red, yellow, and blue. Paint a picture using only colours you mix yourself. How many new colours can you create? |
| 6 | Self Portrait | Look in a mirror. What colour are your eyes? Your hair? Paint a big picture of yourself. Don't forget your favourite outfit! |
| 7 | Sponge Painting | Cut sponges into shapes - stars, circles, hearts. Dip them in paint and stamp a pattern. Make wrapping paper for someone special! |
| 8 | Rainy Day Art | Put drops of food colouring on paper and leave it in the rain for a few minutes. Watch the rain make art! Bring it inside to dry. |
| 9 | Playdough Creations | Make your own playdough (flour, salt, water, oil) with your Big Person. Shape it into your favourite animals and paint them when they dry! |
| 10 | Collage Creation | Cut or tear up old magazines, coloured paper, and fabric scraps. Glue them together to make a picture. Tell the story of your collage! |
| 11 | Pasta Jewellery | Paint pasta tubes in bright colours. When they're dry, thread them onto string to make necklaces and bracelets for everyone! |
| 12 | Finger Painting Feelings | Use your fingers (no brushes!) to paint how you're feeling today. Happy might be bright yellows and swirls! What does excited look like? |
